# Chapter 18 OOP Results

Scope: OOP, defined as OOPG plus NCD OOP converted to monthly terms.

Weighting: household survey weights (`hhs_wt`), matching the household-level Chapter 18 presentation.

Primary denominator convention: reconstructed nominal total consumption plus OOPG for total-expenditure CHE; real nonfood consumption plus OOPG for nonfood CHE.

## Key OOP Findings

- OOP > 10% of total expenditure: 18.47%; overshoot: 3.12%; MPO: 16.90%.
- OOP > 40% of nonfood expenditure: 7.57%; overshoot: 3.57%; MPO: 47.11%.
- Concentration index for OOP > 10% of total expenditure: 0.0781; rank-weighted headcount: 17.03%.
- Concentration index for OOP > 40% of nonfood expenditure: 0.1552; rank-weighted headcount: 6.40%.
